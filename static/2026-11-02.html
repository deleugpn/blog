Amor,

I miss you and I miss us, I miss the life we had, the time we shared, the freedom. On some level I had a small idea that this would be the hardest thing we’d do in life, but never in my life I thought it would be this hard. But I’m glad I get to do it with you. You’re the strongest most decisive and caring person I know. You make me a better person and a better father and you never settle. You deserve the best and you want the best for your kids. I don’t think I’m good enough, but I’ll keep trying. I wanna do better for our kids and for you.

If things are extremely hard for me I can barely understand how hard it is for you and how mentally, emotionally and physically draining it must be. All I can hope for is that you don’t give up on me. They’re going to grow up and it’s going to be a little bit of us again and then a little more of us and finally a lot of just us again. It’s a process that will take 3 versions of us each 7 years and I want every version of you to feel loved, seen, heard and accompanied.

We’ve been through so much together, a difficult start, a rare challenge living abroad, most of it has been good, great or amazing. Now we’re going through some tough moments that can drift us apart, create resentments and generate conflicts. I hope we can do what we always did through these years: communicate, adapt and work together. “Os transtornos passam, os benefícios ficam”.

You’re the best investment I could ever make and I’m grateful everyday for having you in my life. You’re the best part of our kids and the world will be a better place for having 5 pieces of you in it. I love you.

— Marco ♡
